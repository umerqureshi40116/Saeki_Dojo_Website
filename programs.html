Program 01

Youth
Program.

Classes designed for ages 17 and under — a journey towards enhancing your human potential.

Young students build resilience and athletic performance, but the deeper work is attentional. A child who can hold a stance, wait for a count, and bow to a partner they just lost to is learning something no scoreboard measures.

Classes are structured and quiet. Instructors do not shout; they hold the standard and let the repetition do the teaching. Progress is marked by JKA grading, so a belt means the same thing here as it does in Tokyo.

Program 02

Adult
Program.

Learn traditional Japanese karate with our adult classes — develop your skills and reach your full potential.

All ages and all levels train together. Complete beginners stand in the same lines as practitioners with twenty years behind them, which is exactly how it is meant to work: the senior grades set the tempo and the new students learn by proximity.

Evening classes run three nights a week. A midday session on Tuesdays and Thursdays exists for people whose evenings are not their own.

Inside an hour

What a class
actually looks like.

00:00 — 黙想

Mokuso

Kneel, close the eyes, settle. Two minutes that separate the day outside from the hour ahead.

00:05 — 基本

Kihon

Basics up and down the floor. Blocks, strikes, kicks, on a count — the spine of every session.

00:30 — 型

Kata

Formal sequences worked by grade. Beginners drill Heian; seniors go deeper into the same forms.

00:45 — 組手

Kumite

Partner work with controlled contact, then the Dōjō Kun and the bow that closes the class.

Before you come

Reasonable questions.

None. Most of the black belts in the room walked in knowing nothing. Your first few classes are about learning to stand, turn and count — everyone starts there.
Loose clothing you can move in, and bare feet. You do not need a gi until you decide to stay; we will point you to Hirota when the time comes.
Our adult class takes all ages and all levels. Karate scales to the body doing it — you will be working hard, but against your own baseline, not someone else's.
We follow official Japan Karate Association testing requirements. Gradings happen periodically through the year, and the grade you earn is recognised by the JKA internationally.
Controlled contact, introduced gradually. Beginners spend a long time on prearranged drills before any free sparring. Stopping the technique is the skill being taught.
Yes. Drop-in classes are $20 each — try the class before committing to a membership, and pay online or in person by cash or card.
