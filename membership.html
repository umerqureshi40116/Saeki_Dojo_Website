Joining

to the floor.

01

Get in touch

Call, email, or send the form. Tell us the age of the student and which nights suit you.

Try a class free

Come and watch, or borrow a gi and join in. No commitment, no sales conversation afterwards.

Registration form

What we ask for,
and why.

A registration form must be completed before your first class. It is short, and every field on it exists for a reason.

  • 1IdentityName and date of birth
    For JKA records and correct grading division.
  • 2ReachableContact details and address
    Invoices, closures, and emergencies.
  • 3SafetyMedical conditions and allergies
    Instructors need to know before you step on the floor.
  • 4PlacementMartial arts experience
    So we start you in the right place, not the polite place.
  • 5ScheduleProgram selection
    Youth or adult, and which nights you intend to train.
