The dojo

A non-profit dojo built
to protect the real thing.

Ottawa JKA Canada exists to maintain the quality and values of traditional Shotokan karate — not a diluted, commercial version of it.

Our objective is to develop students through intensive training that cultivates mental and physical discipline. What is earned on the dojo floor is meant to leave with you: the composure to overcome challenges and obstacles in real life.

We are a member dojo of the Japan Karate Association, follow official JKA testing requirements, and maintain a direct relationship with JKA Headquarters in Tokyo.

Method

Three pillars.
One practice.

01 — 基本

Kihon

The fundamentals. Stance, transition, and the single correct line a technique travels. Every belt in the room drills the same basics — the difference is only depth.

02 — 型

Kata

Formal sequences preserved unchanged for generations. Kata is the library of Shotokan: a record of intention, timing, and breath that you decode slowly, over years.

03 — 組手

Kumite

Controlled partner work, from prearranged drills to free sparring. Distance, timing, and the discipline to stop — kumite is where composure is actually tested.

Leadership

The people
on the floor.

Hover a portrait to read more.

7th Dan

Minoru Saeki

Founder & Chairman

Opened the dojo in 1973 after arriving in Canada. Licensed to conduct examinations up to 3rd Dan and a member of the JKA Masters Committee in Tokyo. Started the annual summer camp that grew into one of North America's largest karate events.

5th Dan

Seiji Saeki

President & Head Instructor

Trained in Japan from 2013, took 1st place in kumite at the 2014 JKA Kanto Region Championship, and completed the JKA Headquarters instructor programme in 2016 — one of very few non-Japanese to do so.

3rd Dan

Yulia Saeki

Executive Director

Completed a Master's degree at the University of Tsukuba. Works alongside Seiji to build the exchange between Canadian and Japanese dojos that keeps our instruction connected to its source.
